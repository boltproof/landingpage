Learn to run AI on your own hardware

Six free, plain-English guides that take you from zero to a private AI you own. No jargon, no sales pitch in the middle, and every step uses free software.

Last updated 2026-09-21

Most people think running their own AI means buying a server room. It doesn't. If your computer has 16 GB of RAM, you can run a useful AI on it this afternoon, for free, with nobody watching what you ask. The expensive part of AI is training a model. You're not doing that. You're running one, which is just loading a file and asking it questions.

Read these in order, or jump to the one you need. Each ends with the same honest offer: set it up yourself, free, or ask me to do it for you.
