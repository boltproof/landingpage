AI Workflows

The layer that sits on top of your Private AI System: automations for email, leads, scheduling and reporting, priced per workflow.

A Private AI System is the appliance. AI Workflows are what it actually does for your business each day. Every workflow below runs on the same server already in your office, reads only the documents and inboxes you connect, and drops drafts and reports in front of a person before anything goes out.

What we build

AI Inbox

Reads incoming enquiries, drafts a reply using your pricing and policy documents, and creates a follow-up task. A person approves before it sends.

Document Intelligence

Point it at a folder of contracts, policies or manuals. Staff ask questions in plain English or Arabic and get an answer with the source cited.

Lead Processing

New leads from a form, WhatsApp or email are captured, qualified against your criteria and routed to the right person automatically.

Quote Follow-Up

Tracks quotes sent to customers and drafts a follow-up at the interval you choose, so nothing falls through.

Report Generation

Turns invoices, spreadsheets or call logs into a structured report on a schedule — daily, weekly or monthly.

Internal Knowledge Assistant

An "ask the ops manual" assistant that answers staff questions from your actual procedures, not a rigid FAQ bot.

Questions we get asked

Do I need to buy a Private AI System first?
Usually yes, since most workflows need somewhere private to run and to store the documents they read. If you already have compatible infrastructure, tell us and we'll assess it.
What happens to the data the workflow touches?
It stays on your Private AI System unless a step is explicitly configured to use an external service, and you approve that configuration before it goes live.
Can a workflow send things automatically without approval?
Only if you ask us to remove the approval step. By default, every workflow drops its output in an approvals folder for a person to check first.
How long does a workflow take to build?
Most single workflows are delivered within 5 to 10 working days from the fixed quote being agreed.
