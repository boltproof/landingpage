The book · Eli Thomas

Your Data, Your Rules

How to run private AI on your own hardware, keep your data yours, and stop renting your brain from a cloud.

This is a book for people who run a business, not for engineers. If you can set up a printer, you can follow it. I assume you know nothing about AI and less about servers, and I keep the jargon to a minimum.

You already use AI through someone else's website. ChatGPT, Claude, whatever. Every prompt you type gets sent to a server you don't own, in a country you can't name, run by a company whose terms of service you didn't read. Your customer names, your pricing, your contracts, your half-finished product ideas. All of it sitting on someone else's hard drive.

This book shows you how to run the same kind of AI on a machine you already own. Nothing leaves your building. You stop paying per month for the privilege of feeding a stranger your business. If the internet goes down, your AI still works. If the vendor raises prices, changes its rules, or gets breached, you don't care.

What's inside

  • Why "private AI" isn't a niche, and when the cloud is genuinely the better buy.
  • Exactly what hardware you need, and what you don't (no graphics card required).
  • Setting up your first model in minutes, free.
  • Pointing it at your own documents so it stops making things up.
  • Keeping it secure and off the internet.
  • The UAE data-residency angle, in plain terms.
  • An honest closing chapter on doing it yourself versus paying someone like me.

It's a skill book, not a sales pitch. Everything in it works with free, open-source software that costs you nothing to use. It updates as the software moves, and people who buy directly from me get updates free.
