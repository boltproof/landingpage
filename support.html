BoltProof Care

AED 1,000 per month, excluding VAT. No contract. No minimum term. Cancel any time with a WhatsApp message. We keep the unit updated, monitored and backed up, so nobody in your office has to.

What Care includes

  • Proactive monitoring. We see disk space, temperature, failed backups and stalled services before you do.
  • Model and security updates. New model versions tested and rolled out. macOS and stack security patches applied.
  • Monthly backup restore test. We restore a real file from backup every month and confirm it opens. A backup that has never been restored is a guess.
  • Priority WhatsApp support. Reply the same business day.
  • One workflow or automation tweak per month. A new prompt template, a change to an n8n workflow, a new document folder connected.
  • Monthly one-page health report. Uptime, usage, storage, backup status, what changed, what we recommend next.
  • Warranty claim logistics. If hardware fails, we handle the Apple or Synology claim and the restore onto the replacement.

Without Care

The unit works exactly the same. Care is optional and the hardware does not depend on it.

  • macOS security updates still install automatically.
  • Backups still run on the schedule we set at delivery.
  • You keep the cheat sheet, and the onboarding call is included in every package.
  • Support outside Care is available at an hourly rate, quoted on request.
  • Model updates, restore tests and workflow changes are yours to do, or to ask us for as one-off work.

Most customers with more than 5 staff take Care. Most sole practitioners do not. Both are fine.

What is not covered

  • Accidental damage, liquid, power surges and theft, unless AppleCare+ is added for the new Macs.
  • Data. A warranty replaces hardware; it does not bring files back. That is what the Backup & Redundancy add-on is for.
  • Software you or a third party install after delivery.

How a claim works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with what happened.
  2. We confirm whether it is hardware, software or configuration. Most reported failures are not hardware and are fixed remotely the same day.
  3. If it is hardware: BoltProof Care customers hand the unit to us and we handle the Apple or Synology claim, a loan or replacement unit where available, and the restore from backup. Customers without Care take the unit to Apple or the Synology distributor directly; we will tell you where to go and what to say.

AppleCare+ prices in AED, excluding VAT, on request. Final quote confirmed on order; Apple pricing may change.
