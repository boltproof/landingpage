The BoltProof Approved standard

Every unit gets the BoltProof Approved badge only if it passes every check below. This is the trust signal — published, so you know exactly what you're buying.

Hardware checks

  • Chip: M-series Max or Ultra
  • Memory: 64GB minimum
  • Storage: 1TB minimum; 4TB+ preferred
  • AppleCare+: Status verified by serial and stated on every listing
  • Ownership: Activation Lock off, signed out of Find My and iCloud
  • Management: Not enrolled in MDM or Apple Business Manager
  • Battery: Health and cycle count measured and published (laptops)
  • Cosmetics: Grade A or B, no screen marks, dents or pressure spots
  • Reset: Wiped and reinstalled by BoltProof with the latest macOS

Performance checks

  • SSD: Read/write speed verified
  • Ports: Every port tested — Thunderbolt, USB-C, HDMI, SDXC, MagSafe
  • Keyboard, trackpad, speakers, camera: All tested
  • LLM benchmark: 8B, 32B, and 70B models (where memory allows), 3 runs each, median reported
  • Concurrency: 4 simultaneous requests on a 32B model
  • Sustained load: 20 minutes of continuous inference — no throttling or thermal faults
  • Every result published: You see the exact tokens/sec this unit achieved

Tiers: what can it run?

A unit belongs to the tier its memory earns, not its chassis. A 128GB MacBook Pro is Surge. A 64GB Mac Studio is also Surge. “Comfortably” means at least ~5 tokens/sec on that model class — roughly reading speed.

Spark

Up to ~30B models

Mac mini M4 Pro · 48–64GB. Home users, 1–3 person offices, a quiet always-on assistant.

Surge

Up to 70B models

MacBook Pro 16″ M3 Max · 64–128GB. Consultants and small teams who want private AI they can carry.

Storm

The largest open models (200B–600B+ quantised)

Mac Studio M3 Ultra · 256–512GB. Firms that want near-frontier capability entirely on site.
