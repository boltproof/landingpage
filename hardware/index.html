The BoltProof Approved standard

A unit only gets the badge if it passes every check below.

Hardware

  • ChipM-series Max or Ultra
  • Memory64GB minimum
  • Storage1TB minimum, 4TB+ preferred
  • CoverageAppleCare+ status verified by serial
  • OwnershipActivation Lock off, Find My & iCloud signed out
  • ManagementNot enrolled in MDM or Apple Business Manager
  • BatteryHealth and cycle count published
  • CosmeticsGrade A or B — no screen marks or dents
  • ResetWiped and reinstalled with the latest macOS

Performance

  • SSDRead/write speed verified
  • PortsThunderbolt, USB-C, HDMI, SDXC, MagSafe tested
  • I/OKeyboard, trackpad, speakers, camera tested
  • LLM benchmark8B, 32B and 70B models, 3 runs, median reported
  • Concurrency4 simultaneous requests on a 32B model
  • Sustained load20 minutes of inference, no throttling
  • PublishedThe tokens/sec this exact unit achieved

What can it run?

Memory decides the tier, not the chassis. “Comfortably” means at least ~5 tokens/sec — roughly reading speed.

Questions people ask

Are these new or used?

Pre-owned, but verified, tested, wiped and reinstalled by BoltProof. Where a unit has active AppleCare+, Apple covers repairs and replacement at any Apple Store or authorised service provider in the UAE. Each listing states its coverage.

Why buy from BoltProof instead of a used-Mac shop?

You know what the machine can run before you buy it. Every unit is benchmarked on real local-AI workloads and the result is published — not just the spec sheet.

Can I extend AppleCare+?

On units that have it, yes — directly with Apple. We share the serial so you can verify the end date on checkcoverage.apple.com before you buy.

What if something breaks?

AppleCare+ units are covered by Apple. For units without it, we diagnose and arrange the repair for you.

Do you deliver?

Across the UAE. We can also hand it over as a complete private AI system — models installed, document search configured, staff trained. See how that works.
