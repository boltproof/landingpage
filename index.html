How it works

One private AI system, running in your office.

The AI runs on-site. Staff connect through a private encrypted network, with remote support available when configured. A NAS keeps a backup copy. There's no port forwarding and no public-facing IP — the Mac is reachable only by devices your Tailscale network has explicitly authorized.

Local vs cloud

Honest answer: sometimes ChatGPT is the better buy.

ChatGPT gives you an AI service. BoltProof gives your business a private AI system configured around its own information and workflows.

Under five people, with little confidential data and mostly general-purpose work? A public AI subscription may be the simpler choice. If your team handles confidential client, patient or financial data, or your workflows involve substantial document processing and repetitive administration, local or hybrid AI may make sense — team size is one factor in that decision, not the deciding one.
