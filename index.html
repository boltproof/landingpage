Services

Everything your Synology needs — from first boot to forever

Whether you're setting up your first NAS or managing a fleet across multiple offices, we handle the full lifecycle so you can focus on your business.

Synology Setup & Lifecycle

Hardware selection, unboxing, RAID configuration, storage pool design, DSM installation, and initial deployment. We handle model selection, expansion planning, and DSM updates so your NAS runs optimally from day one through year ten.

Backup & Recovery Design

3-2-1 backup strategy, snapshot scheduling, versioning retention, off-site replication, and disaster recovery runbooks. We design multi-layered protection so you can restore any file — or your entire system — in minutes, not days.

Networking & Secure Remote Access

VLAN segmentation, Tailscale/WireGuard VPN tunnels, DNS configuration, reverse proxies, and secure remote access for distributed teams. Access your NAS from anywhere without exposing it to the internet.

Security Hardening

Firewall rules, 2FA enforcement, certificate management, DSM security audit, vulnerability patching, access control reviews, and ransomware-resistant architecture. We lock down your NAS against the threats that matter.

Ongoing Care Retainer

Monthly health checks, DSM updates, security patches, backup verification, capacity planning, and priority support. Your NAS gets the attention of a dedicated specialist without the cost of a full-time hire.

Hardware Consultation

Model selection, capacity planning, drive recommendations (HDD vs NVMe caching), expansion units, and upgrade paths. We help you buy the right hardware the first time — no costly re-purchases or underpowered setups.
