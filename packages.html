What every package includes

The BoltProof AI Stack

  • Ollama: the engine that runs AI models on the Mac itself.
  • Open WebUI: a chat interface that works like ChatGPT, with a login for each staff member, private to your business.
  • Document Q&A (RAG): point it at a folder of your files. Ask questions. Get answers with the source page cited.
  • n8n: builds automations and agent workflows, for example reading incoming invoices and filing the totals into a spreadsheet.
  • Tailscale: secure, authenticated access from staff laptops and phones, in the office or not. No port forwarding, no public-facing IP — the Mac isn't reachable from the open internet, only from devices your Tailscale network has authorized.

Set-up and handover

  • macOS hardened: FileVault disk encryption, firewall on, automatic security updates, separate admin account.
  • Runs headless. No monitor, keyboard or desk needed. It sits on a shelf next to the router.
  • Optional Time Machine and Synology backup, configured before delivery.
  • Models downloaded, tested and ready. The first answer works on day one.
  • 90-minute onboarding call with your team.
  • Printed one-page cheat sheet: how to log in, how to ask, what to do if something looks wrong.

Add-on: Backup & Redundancy

From AED 4,500

Synology 2-bay NAS, two mirrored drives

A small box that sits next to the Mac and keeps a second copy of everything. We configure it before delivery.

  • Two mirrored drives. Each drive holds an identical copy. If one drive fails, the other still has everything. You replace the failed drive and it rebuilds itself.
  • Nightly backup of the Mac: your documents, the chat history, the model settings and the automations.
  • Versioned snapshots. If a file is deleted or overwritten on Tuesday, you can get Monday's version back.
  • Off-site copy option. An encrypted copy to a second location or to a cloud storage bucket you control, for fire, theft or flood.

What redundancy means in practice: if the Mac dies, we restore your data and the full configuration onto a replacement in hours. Without it, you are starting from scratch and rebuilding from whatever copies staff happen to have.

Questions we get asked

Does any of my data go to OpenAI, Google or anyone else?
Not by default. The models that answer your questions run on the Mac in your office, not in a cloud AI service. The network traffic that does exist is: encrypted Tailscale access for your own staff (and for BoltProof if you take Care support), software updates, and — if you add it — the encrypted off-site backup copy. If you later choose to switch on a cloud model for non-sensitive work, that's a separate option you turn on yourself with your own API key.
Is a local model as good as ChatGPT?
For questions over your own documents, drafting, summarising and translation, the 70B-class and larger models are close, and often better because they see your files. For general knowledge questions, the frontier cloud models are still ahead. Most small businesses buy this for the first category.
Do we need a monitor for it?
No. It runs headless. Staff use it from a browser on their own laptop or phone.
Can staff working from home use it?
Yes. Tailscale gives each staff member a secure, authenticated connection from their own device — no ports opened on your router, no VPN box to manage, and the Mac stays unreachable from the open internet to anyone outside that network.
How many people can use it at the same time?
AI Desk: 1 to 3. AI Workhorse: 3 to 8. AI Powerhouse: 10 or more. More people can be logged in; those numbers are how many can be waiting for an answer at the same moment before it slows down.
What if we outgrow it?
The stack and your documents move to a bigger unit. We migrate it for you. The old unit becomes a backup or a second server.
Can you connect our accounting or practice management software?
Often, through n8n, if the software has an export or an API. Tell us what you use and we will say yes or no before you order.
Why is the Workhorse pre-owned?
The M1 Ultra with 128 GB is no longer sold new by Apple. It still runs 70B models well and costs less than a new Mac with the same memory. It is fully refurbished and carries a BoltProof 12-month hardware warranty.
