Questions people ask

Do you do home visits or just businesses?

Both. A home user with a slow Mac is just as welcome as a business that needs a private AI system. No job too small — if it's one laptop, it's one laptop.

Can you recover a Mac that won't start up?

Usually, yes. I'll diagnose it on the spot — disk, logic board, OS corruption — and tell you honestly whether it's fixable or time to replace. If there's data on it, I'll recover it first.

What if I'm not in Dubai?

On site across the UAE. If you're further out, or overseas, I can often help remotely over SSH — you don't need to be in the same room as the machine for most server and NAS work.

Do you sell hardware?

I source and supply hardware when you need it — Macs, servers, NAS units, networking gear. But I'm not a shop. I'm the person who sets it up, fixes it, and makes sure it runs. Book an assessment and we'll figure out what fits.

How much does it cost?

Quick fixes are quoted up front — you know the price before I start. Larger setups and AI projects are scoped and quoted in writing. No hourly rates, no surprises, no open-ended invoices.

Got something that needs fixing?

WhatsApp me. If it's quick, I'll sort it today. If it's a project, I'll come see you and write you a quote.
